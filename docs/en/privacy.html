FiveQ (iPhone, Apple Watch) and FiveQThoughts (Mac, Windows) do not collect any personal data for us. There is no tracking, no analytics, no cookies, no advertising and no backend server of our own.

4. What data do the apps (FiveQ & FiveQThoughts) process?

  • API key: Your TheBrain API key is stored exclusively locally on your device (iPhone/Apple Watch: app settings; desktop: local configuration file).
  • Quick Thoughts: Text, links and images you create, edit or view are transferred directly between your device and TheBrain – either your local TheBrain desktop app (localhost) or TheBrain Cloud (api.bra.in). TheBrain Technologies LP's privacy policy applies to processing there.
  • Dictation: Dictation on iPhone and Apple Watch is processed by Apple's system speech recognition (see Apple's privacy information).
  • Apple Watch: The API key is transferred directly from your iPhone to your paired Apple Watch via Apple's WatchConnectivity.

5. Contact by email

If you contact us by email, the data you provide (name, email address, message content) will be processed solely to handle your inquiry (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). It will not be shared with third parties and will be deleted once your inquiry has been resolved, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

To exercise these rights, please contact info@dls-software.de.
